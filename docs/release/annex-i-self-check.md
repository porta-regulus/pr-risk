## 1. What this document is

1. This is a voluntary self-check (SPEC.md Sec. 11 item 8). ADR-0003 finds that the app is a free tool with no monetisation, kept so by its conditions C1 to C7, and that PortaRegulus does not place it on the market. So Annex I does not apply as a legal duty. PortaRegulus applies the measures of Art. 24 on a voluntary basis, and this document does not claim the status of an open-source software steward. PortaRegulus also sells CVD Portal. Counsel confirmed the finding of ADR-0003 against Recitals 15 and 18, with one condition. PortaRegulus does not market or bundle the app as a feature of CVD Portal or as an inducement to buy it (section 5, question 3).
2. This document does not claim conformity. There is no EU declaration of conformity and no CE marking. It uses no harmonised standard, so it claims no presumption of conformity under Art. 27.
3. The cybersecurity risk assessment of Art. 13(2) is the STRIDE threat model in ADR-0004. Each row below names the threat IDs of that model.
4. The check covers the hosted build on `https://risk.portaregulus.com` and the release bundle. Both are the same files (ADR-0004 decision 2).

## 5. Questions for counsel and their conclusions

Counsel answered each question before the sign-off. Only the conclusions of counsel are in this document. They were recorded on 2026-11-06.

1. Point I 2(c). Does a permanent opt-out for one user apply to the hosted app, read with the words "where applicable" in 2(c)? The hosted app has one public origin with no account, and the origin serves the current release to every new session. The open tabs keep their version until the user closes them all. Self-hosting is a separate deployment option for teams that need a fixed version.
   Conclusion. Confirmed. The words "where applicable" in the first sentence of Part I point (2) and in point 2(c) apply. A permanent opt-out for one user does not apply to the single public origin with no account. To keep an old version for one user, the public origin must serve old builds, and that does not agree with an app that has no account and no telemetry. The open tabs postpone an update until they all close, the notices tell the user about updates, and self-hosting is a separate option for a fixed version (`docs/self-host.md`). Point I 2(c) is Met.
2. Point I 2(l). Can 2(l) be Met when read access and the opt-out do not apply (F6)? The missing author of F14 is a separate gap.
   Conclusion. Confirmed. The first sentence of Part I point (2) says "On the basis of the cybersecurity risk assessment referred to in Article 13(2) and where applicable". A record of read access and an opt-out from the change log do not apply. The app has one user, keeps its data in the browser, and has no server and no access control for many users. A record of each read in the register file gives no protection against access from outside (ADR-0004), and it records personal activity that the app does not need, against data minimisation in point 2(g). An opt-out from the change log does not apply, because the change log is the integrity record of the document (ADR-0004 R1) and sends no data. Apart from gap F14, which is accepted for release 1, point I 2(l) is Met.
3. Section 1 item 1 and Art. 24. Confirm against Recital 15 and Art. 3(22) that PortaRegulus does not supply the app "in the course of a commercial activity". A company hosts the free app on its own domain, https://risk.portaregulus.com. ADR-0003 conditions C1 to C6 keep the app free of a price, an account, telemetry, a paid feature and an in-app sales link to CVD Portal. PortaRegulus also sells CVD Portal, and the app imports a CVD Portal export (SPEC.md Sec. 9.5).
   Conclusion. Confirmed, with one condition. Counsel read Art. 3(18), Art. 3(22) and Recitals 15 and 18. Recital 18 makes the test product by product. While PortaRegulus keeps conditions C1 to C6 of ADR-0003, and while it does not market or bundle the app as a feature of CVD Portal or as an inducement to buy it, no sign of monetisation of Recital 15 applies to the app. The import of a CVD Portal export from a local file is technical interoperability, not monetisation.
   Condition. The condition about marketing and bundles goes further than C3, which covers only links in the app. The product owner added it to ADR-0003 as condition C7 on 2026-11-06.
