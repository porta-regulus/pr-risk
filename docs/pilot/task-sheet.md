# Task sheet

You are the IT lead of Example Valves GmbH. The company is fictitious. Your managing directors want a decision record for the 2027 budget. They will approve which risk responses to fund and which risks to accept.

Use the app on the screen. Start with "Guided mode". Use the help text in the app when you need it. A "?" opens an explanation. Say aloud what you think.

## The company

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Business | Makes industrial valves and sells spare parts in a web shop |
| Staff | 140 |
| Turnover | EUR 28 M per year |
| NIS2 | Important entity (manufacturing, Annex II) |
| IT | 3 staff. An MSP runs the servers. The ERP is a cloud service. |
| Money the company can lose in one year without a threat to payroll | EUR 2 M (cash reserve and the unused credit line) |
| Budget for risk responses in 2027 | EUR 70 000 first-year cash |

## What happened in the last years

1. In 2024 and 2025, ransomware hit two companies in your industry group. One stopped production for 9 days.
2. In 2025, a fake supplier invoice made the company pay EUR 18 000 to a fraudster. In 2023 a similar attempt was found in time.
3. The web shop was down 3 times in 2 years because the hosting provider had an outage. Each outage cost between EUR 2 000 and EUR 15 000 in lost orders.
4. The MSP has admin access to all servers. A breach at the MSP would reach the ERP and the file servers.
5. The sales lead thinks that a supplier portal with online ordering could win new wholesale customers.

You decide the ranges. There is no correct answer. Give ranges that you are 90% sure of.

## The task

1. Create a document for Example Valves GmbH.
2. Add at least 2 objectives of the company.
3. Add at least 5 risks.
   1. At least 1 risk from the catalogue in the app.
   2. At least 1 risk that you write yourself.
   3. At least 1 positive risk (an opportunity).
   4. Each risk has a frequency range, an impact range, an owner and an objective.
4. Add at least 1 driver (a shared cause) and link it to 2 risks.
5. Set a tolerance curve with 3 or more points.
6. Add at least 2 response options with their costs and effects.
7. Find the best portfolio within the budget.
8. Print the decision record, or save it as PDF.

Tell the facilitator when the record is printed.
