# Consent to take part in a usability session

PortaRegulus tests a free risk register app, PortaRegulus Risk. We ask you to do one task with the app while you say aloud what you think. We test the app, not you. This text is for the pilot session and for the AT-5 sessions.

## What happens

1. You do the task on the task sheet. It takes 45 minutes at most.
2. The facilitator cannot help you and does not answer questions during the task. The help text in the app is allowed.
3. We record the screen and your voice. We do not record your face.
4. After the task, we ask you a few short questions.

## Your data

1. The app stores all data in this browser only. It sends no data to a server and has no tracking.
2. The company in the task is fictitious. Do not enter data of your own company.
3. We use the recording and our notes only to find problems in the app and to measure the time of the task. The legal basis is your consent (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Article 6(1), point (a)).
4. Only the facilitator and the product owner of PortaRegulus see the recording. We store it encrypted and do not give it to other parties.
5. We keep the recording for 90 days at most, then we delete it.
6. Our notes name your role, the size band of your company and its sector. They do not name you or your company.
7. You can stop at any time without a reason. If you stop, we delete the recording at once if you ask.
8. You can ask us for a copy of your data, a correction or a deletion. You can withdraw your consent at any time. A withdrawal does not make the use before it unlawful. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
