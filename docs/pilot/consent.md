# Consent to take part in a pilot session

PortaRegulus tests a free risk register app, PortaRegulus Risk. We ask you to do one task with the app while you say aloud what you think. We test the app, not you.

## What happens

1. You do the task on the task sheet. It takes up to 30 minutes.
2. We record the screen and your voice. We do not record your face.
3. After the task, we ask you 3 short questions.

## Your data

1. The app stores all data in this browser only. It sends no data to a server and has no tracking.
2. The company in the task is fictitious. Do not enter data of your own company.
3. We keep the recording for 90 days at most, then we delete it.
4. Our notes name your role, the size band of your company and its sector. They do not name you or your company.
5. You can stop at any time without a reason. If you stop, we delete the recording at once if you ask.

## Agreement

I have read this text. I agree to take part and to the screen and voice recording.
