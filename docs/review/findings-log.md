# AT-6 findings log

One log for both reviewers. The terms are in [checklist.md](checklist.md) Sec. 1.

## Rules

1. Write one finding in each row.
2. Give each finding an ID. Use `C-01`, `C-02` and so on for the CISO, and `A-01`, `A-02` and so on for the auditor.
3. Name the exact place, for example the file ID of the material list, the page, the section number and the field.
4. Quote at most a short phrase of the material or of a legal text.
5. Only the reviewer who wrote a finding can change its severity or close it.
6. A blocking finding stays open until the reviewer who wrote it checks the fix on new material.
7. A question that the help text in the app does not answer is a finding too.
